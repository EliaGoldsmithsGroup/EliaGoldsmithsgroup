Knowledge & Education

Precious Metals & Investment

Understand the products before making a financial decision.

Gold

Gold is traded globally and is often considered by investors seeking to diversify their holdings. Its market price can rise or fall, and physical products may include additional costs.

Silver

Silver has both investment and industrial uses. Its market price can be volatile, and physical silver products may involve premiums, storage considerations and transaction costs.

Know the Risks

Precious metals do not guarantee a profit. Prices can fall, selling costs may apply and it may take time to find a buyer. Research carefully and consider independent financial advice.
